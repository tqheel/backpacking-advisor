⚠️ Important Reminders

SCOUTS PACK THEIR OWN GEAR: Use this checklist yourself — don't ask a parent to pack for you. This is your job.

LABEL YOUR GEAR (YOURSELF): Write your own name on every bag, stuff sack, and piece of gear before it goes in the trailer

LAYERS: April nights at Camp Reeves get cold — dress in layers and bring your warm hat and gloves

FOOD SAFETY: Keep food in sealed containers or coolers — never leave food out overnight

LEAVE NO TRACE: Pack out all trash, every wrapper, every scrap — leave the campsite better than you found it

DO NOT BRING: Video games, tablets, expensive electronics, or valuables you'd be heartbroken to lose
